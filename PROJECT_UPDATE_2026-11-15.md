# Project update — November 15, 2026

This addendum records the music-related storytelling decisions confirmed on November 15. It supplements the current forest-first experience plan and the canonical project brief.

## Songs and personal context

Ghala loves two songs by Saudi singer Mohammed Abdu:
- **“Muthhila”** — the creator and Ghala listened to this together recently. She especially loves the romantic lyrics and the ending passage.
- **“Ma Tmaneitak”** — another favourite that may inspire a separate lily discovery.

The romantic meaning of the lyrics is the main reason these songs resonate with her, while Mohammed Abdu's vocal delivery is also important.

## Confirmed Muthhila lily

One of the five special lilies in the Lily Clearing should be dedicated to “Muthhila.”

When she opens it:
1. The lily blooms.
2. A selected passage from the song's original Arabic lyrics appears as the main discovery message.
3. Arabic is presented first and remains the focus, using correct RTL direction/alignment, readable spacing, and an elegant Arabic typeface.
4. An English translation is optional and secondary.
5. A separate personal note written or approved by the creator may appear after or alongside the lyric passage, visually distinguished from the song lyrics.

The creator wants the lyric reference to remain recognisable and preserve its original wording rather than being lightly rewritten.

## Optional audio

- Song playback is optional and user-initiated, via a discreet play/replay control.
- Do not autoplay the song when the lily opens.
- Use an authorised embed or properly licensed source for the actual recording; do not download or self-host an unauthorised copy.
- The lyric reveal must remain complete and meaningful even if playback is unavailable, blocked, or declined.
- Check browser playback restrictions and the selected provider's embedding terms during implementation.

## Ma Tmaneitak

“Ma Tmaneitak” may inspire another of the five special lilies. The exact lyric passage and personal note are not selected yet; do not invent or finalise them.

## Implementation status

These are planning decisions, not completed features. No lyric display, lily interaction, or song playback has been implemented or tested. Full lyric text is deliberately not included in public project documentation; exact excerpts will be selected/approved during implementation.
