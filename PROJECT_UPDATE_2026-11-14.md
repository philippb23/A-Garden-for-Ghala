# Project update — November 14, 2026

This is a dated addendum to `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`. Where this update conflicts with the earlier first-visit progression or forest priority in that file, **this November 14 decision supersedes it**.

## Confirmed experience and navigation

The **Forgotten Forest is the starting location after the welcome screen**. Do not send the visitor directly to the lily clearing and do not insert a Garden hub between the welcome screen and the forest.

First-visit flow:

`Welcome screen → Forgotten Forest → explore one of the forest paths`

The forest is not an optional stretch goal anymore. It is the opening environment and the initial navigation hub. Its mood should be mysterious, unfamiliar, slightly spooky, and Twilight-inspired: dense Pacific Northwest evergreens, cool blue-green/deep navy tones, mist, moss, and soft diffused light. The inspiration is atmospheric only; do not reproduce Twilight film stills, copyrighted compositions, or character likenesses.

The forest's existing visual concept of a large/central tree and **two distinct paths** remains important:
- **Path of Light** leads toward the warm, welcoming lily clearing.
- **Path of Whispers** leads toward the coastal/beach area.

These are planned destinations; the second path still needs implementation. Keep navigation exploratory rather than turning the site into a rigid slideshow. The visitor should be able to return to the forest and revisit discovered locations. A separate Garden hub is not part of the current plan.

## Core discovery sequence

1. Welcome screen.
2. Forgotten Forest as the opening scene and navigation hub.
3. Lily Clearing, reached by following the warm/light path. It contains abundant realistic pink lilies; every lily can bloom, while five hidden special lilies reveal creator-written personal messages.
4. Finding all five special lilies unlocks the garden puzzle.
5. Solving the puzzle opens the surprise route to Leo's secluded clearing. Leo's gentle, repeatable petting and purring interaction remains part of this scene.
6. Beach/coast, reached through the forest's second path, with sunset/coastal atmosphere and small discoveries such as shells and birds.

The forest and beach should remain revisitable. Leo's clearing stays locked until the puzzle is solved.

## Storytelling and emotional design principle

The gift should feel thoughtful and specifically made for its recipient, not like a generic romantic website or a collection of decorative effects. Prioritize meaningful personal details, memories and interests that the creator can genuinely stand behind. Let discoveries unfold gradually and at the visitor's own pace. The creator will write or approve all personal messages; do not invent shared memories or generic declarations to fill space.

The contrast between environments is intentional:
- Forest: cool, mysterious, quiet, and slightly eerie.
- Lily Clearing: warm, welcoming, peaceful, and filled with pink lilies.
- Leo's Clearing: intimate, gentle, and comforting.
- Beach: open, peaceful, and atmospheric.

The aim is a complete, polished core experience. If time becomes tight, simplify optional details rather than rushing or leaving the central interactions unfinished.
